About Me 👋

A student building a helpful corner of the internet for other students.

📚

Hey, I'm Devansh!

I'm an ICSE Class 9 student and a JEE aspirant who enjoys learning Maths and Science. I'm also learning how to build useful things online.

I created StudySprint to make revision feel more organised and less overwhelming. The idea is simple: clear notes, quick quizzes, useful study tools, and steady progress—one topic at a time.

This is a student-made project, so it will keep improving as I learn and add more content. If you're studying too, I hope StudySprint helps you get started and keep going.

Progress over perfection.
